모션앤닷 (the "Operator") establishes and discloses this Privacy Policy to protect the personal information of users of GIFdot (the "App") and to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

1. Principles of Processing

  • The App's core functions, including media selection, conversion/editing, and saving, are generally performed on the user's device.
  • The operator does not upload and store users' original photo or video files on a separate server for the App's core functions.
  • However, certain information may be processed automatically through external service providers such as Firebase Analytics, Google Mobile Ads SDK, and RevenueCat in order to operate the service, improve quality, serve ads, and manage purchases and subscriptions.
  • If a user separately consents to receive promotional information, information related to marketing notifications may be processed through Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), Apple Push Notification service (APNs), and Cloudflare.
  • If a user participates in Friend Invitations, Cloudflare (Workers, D1, Queues) and relevant Apple and Google services may process information to verify invitation relationships, first GIF or MP4 conversion completion, reward status, and referral-reward notifications.
  • If a user consents to send error diagnostic information, a crash report may be processed through Cloudflare to improve App stability.
  • If a user contacts the operator through "Contact" or customer support, a minimal amount of personal information may be processed to the extent necessary to handle the inquiry.

2. Purposes of Processing Personal Information

The operator may process personal information or information that may constitute personal information for the following purposes:

  • Analyzing app usage and improving the service
  • Analyzing errors, crashes, and performance issues and improving stability
  • Serving ads, measuring ad performance, and preventing abuse
  • Checking purchase and subscription status, validating receipts, providing Pro features, and analyzing purchases
  • Sending promotional app push notifications, including GIFdot discounts, new features, and usage tips, based on separate opt-in consent
  • Managing promotional notification consent and withdrawal records, classifying eligible recipients, and preventing misdelivery and abuse
  • Verifying Friend Invitation relationships, first GIF or MP4 conversion completion, reward issuance, registration, duplicate prevention, and reward notifications
  • Receiving and responding to customer inquiries
  • Responding to disputes and fulfilling legal obligations where necessary

3. Categories of Personal Information Processed

(1) Information that may be processed automatically during use of the App

The operator may automatically process the following information for app operation, quality improvement, and ad delivery:

  • Device identifiers and app instance identifiers
  • App usage information, such as screen or feature interactions
  • Advertising-related data
  • Purchase and subscription history, product and transaction identifiers, price, and currency information
  • Approximate location information
  • Crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data

Note: The above information may be processed through third-party SDKs such as Firebase Analytics, Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), and RevenueCat.

App versions that include the PostHog SDK send a pseudonymous identifier generated for each app installation, app, operating-system and device information, and screen, feature, conversion, saving and purchase events to PostHog US Cloud for usage analysis. This integration does not send original photos or videos, caption or watermark content, raw transaction identifiers or push tokens, and does not use session replay or automatic screen capture. IP-based location enrichment is disabled, but IP addresses and request information may still be processed when handling network requests.

Note: iOS app versions that include tracking permission requests use Apple's AppTrackingTransparency (ATT) prompt. If you allow tracking and the applicable regional privacy consent requirements are met, the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) may use device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA), with data from other companies' apps and websites to deliver personalized ads and measure ad effectiveness.

You can use the App's editing and saving features without allowing tracking. Non-personalized ads may still be shown depending on your privacy consent choices. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Where required, regional ad privacy choices can also be changed through the ad privacy options in the App's settings. These choices are separate from consent to promotional push notifications. Earlier iOS versions without ATT requests retain non-personalized ad settings.

(2) When a user consents to promotional information

  • App push registration tokens (FCM/APNs) and a hashed installation identifier
  • Promotional notification consent or withdrawal status and timestamps, and nighttime notification consent or withdrawal status and timestamps
  • App language, recipient category (free use or purchase/subscription type), app version, and consent notice version
  • App integrity verification information, IP address, and request records

Note: The app installation identifier is stored as a hash in the operator's notification server database. The original FCM/APNs registration tokens for general marketing notifications are not stored in the operator's notification server database; they are processed by Google's and Apple's push notification services to deliver notifications.

Note: Promotional notifications between 9:00 p.m. and 8:00 a.m. the following day are sent only if the user separately consents to receive nighttime notifications.

(3) When a user consents to Friend Invitation reward notifications

  • Hashed installation identifier, original FCM token, enabled status, confirmation and update timestamps, and sending status for the Friend Invitation reward-notification target
  • APNs token and notification payload for Friend Invitation notifications

Note: Friend Invitation reward notifications are managed through GIFdot's notification permission in iOS Settings; the App does not provide a separate in-app off switch. The necessary information may be stored in Cloudflare D1 for the Friend Invitation notification target. Apple account IDs, names, email addresses, and original photos or videos are not stored.

(4) When a user consents to send error diagnostic information

  • Diagnostic report time, signal, exception and termination reason, and error call information
  • App version and build, operating-system version, device type, and transmission consent type
  • App integrity verification information, IP address, and request records

Note: Photos, videos, edit content, account information, and persistent device identifiers are not included in these crash reports.

(5) When a user contacts support

  • Required: Email address, inquiry details
  • Optional: Attachments voluntarily provided by the user, such as screenshots, logs, or conversion outputs

Note: Attachments may include device information or file metadata. Users should review materials before sending them to avoid including sensitive information.

(6) Information that may be processed for Friend Invitations

  • Pseudonymous participant identifier (a hash derived from the Apple AppTransaction identifier and App bundle ID), invitation code, and invitation relationship
  • Event ID, creation and completion timestamps, and conversion-result format for the first GIF or MP4 conversion
  • Reward status, hashed voucher-code and batch metadata, expiration and assignment status, and reward registration, scheduling, and redemption confirmation
  • Original App acquisition time, Apple transaction and product identifiers needed to confirm rewards, verification public keys, and notification delivery status
  • Signed evidence, key identifiers, and verification counters needed to verify Apple AppTransaction, Transaction, and App Attest proofs
  • Hashed installation identifier, original FCM token, enabled status, confirmation and update timestamps, and delivery status for a Friend Invitation notification target. APNs tokens are processed by Apple's and Google's push services.

Note: Apple account IDs, Apple passwords, names, email addresses, original photos or videos, and edit files are not collected for Friend Invitation processing. Information processed by Apple, Google, and Cloudflare as part of their services is subject to each provider's policy.

4. Retention and Use Period

The operator deletes personal information without delay once the purpose of processing has been achieved.

  • Information related to customer inquiries (email, inquiry content, attachments): retained for 1 year after the inquiry is resolved, then deleted
  • Information automatically processed through external SDKs: except for operator-held information described separately below, processed by external providers for periods determined by their policies and applicable law
  • Active marketing notification subscription information: deleted when consent withdrawal or notification-permission revocation is reflected on the server, or when the processing purpose has been achieved
  • Promotional notification consent and withdrawal records: retained for 3 years from the date each record is received by the server for legal compliance and dispute resolution, then deleted
  • Marketing FCM/APNs tokens: not stored in marketing subscription records. When marketing consent is withdrawn, the App requests topic unsubscription and token deletion. External processing follows each provider's policy.
  • Friend Invitation FCM tokens: deleted and the notification target disabled when the App detects revoked notification permission or a stopped campaign and sends an authenticated withdrawal request. These requests are accepted even while the campaign is off. Tokens older than 30 days since their last successful registration or refresh are excluded from delivery and deleted by daily cleanup (normally within 31 days of the last refresh). Tokens confirmed invalid by the delivery service are also deleted. Revocation may not reach the server immediately while the device is offline or the App is not reopened. Installation-to-participant links and delivery records, excluding the token, follow the retention rule below.
  • Friend Invitation relationships, first-conversion, reward, code-assignment, and transaction-confirmation records: retained while needed to operate the campaign, process earned benefits, and prevent duplicate rewards. Withdrawing Friend Invitation notifications or disabling a token does not by itself delete existing ledger records; they may remain until those purposes end. Individual deletion requests may be submitted by email and are reviewed for handling.
  • Error diagnostic information sent with consent: retained for 30 days from server receipt, then deleted by the daily cleanup process after that period (within 31 days of receipt)

However, if retention is required by applicable law, such information may be retained for the period prescribed by that law.
